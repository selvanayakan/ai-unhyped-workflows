Subject: Need help - our delivery process is a mess

Hi, I run BrightRoute Logistics in Pune. We're about 40 people, mostly drivers, plus 6 office staff. Most of our work now is last-mile delivery for US online sellers who ship stock to our warehouse, and some local Pune shops. Right now orders come in from everywhere: some sellers send a WhatsApp message, some email a spreadsheet, and the local shops just call. My office team retypes everything into one big Excel sheet every morning, and mistakes happen - last week we sent 12 parcels to old addresses. Drivers get their list printed at 9 am. They call customers to confirm the address and time, which takes ages, and if the customer doesn't pick up they just come back with the parcel. Nobody updates the sheet until the evening, so when a US seller emails asking where is my order, we honestly don't know until night. With the time difference, they often wait a full day for an answer. Invoicing is the worst part. At month end my accountant goes through the Excel sheet line by line to bill each seller, in USD for the US sellers and INR for the local shops. It takes her about a week and we've had two sellers dispute charges this quarter. We use WhatsApp, Gmail, Excel and Tally. I don't have a big budget, maybe a few hundred dollars a month. I want to automate this somehow, but I don't know where to start. Thanks, Rakesh Kulkarni, Owner, BrightRoute Logistics
